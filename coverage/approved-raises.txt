# The raises of coverage floors that a change records (#297,
# CONTRIBUTING.md, "The coverage floors"). The coverage ratchet
# (scripts/coverage-ratchet.py, in CI's job coverage) compares each
# package's floor in coverage/floors.txt with its floor at the merge base
# with main. A floor above the base's, or a first floor above 0 for a
# package that had none there, fails the ratchet unless the change adds a
# line here for that package:
#
#   <package> <points> <reason>
#
# <package> is the package's directory, as coverage/floors.txt names it;
# <points> is how many points the floor rises, which the ratchet's message
# gives; <reason> says why the points cannot be covered now, such as the
# issue that will cover them. Only the lines that a change adds record its
# raises, and those of a package must add up to at least its raise. A line
# is not the approval, since every commit here has the same author: the
# weakening report lists it with the floors it raises, and the maintainer's
# acknowledgment of that report at the merge is the approval. The lines
# stay, as the record of what was approved.
